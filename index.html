Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 27, 2026

Indo Japan Group ("we", "our", or "us") operates a WhatsApp-based enquiry assistant to help prospective tenants and business partners get information about our commercial properties. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information shared through our WhatsApp chatbot and related services.

Information We Collect

When you interact with our WhatsApp chatbot, we may collect the following information:

Your WhatsApp phone number, the content of messages you send to us, your name and contact details if you voluntarily provide them, and your property enquiry preferences such as size, location, and budget requirements.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your property enquiries, follow up on your interest in our commercial spaces, and improve the quality of our customer service. We do not use your information for marketing purposes without your consent.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law or as necessary to operate our services (such as our technology service providers who help run the chatbot). All third-party providers are contractually obligated to keep your information confidential.

Data Retention

We retain conversation data only as long as necessary to fulfil your enquiry or as required by applicable law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

WhatsApp Messaging

Our WhatsApp chatbot is powered by Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. By messaging us on WhatsApp, you agree to Meta's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy in addition to this policy. We only send messages in response to your enquiries and do not initiate unsolicited contact.

Your Rights

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate data, request deletion of your data, and opt out of future communications at any time by sending "STOP" to our WhatsApp number.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure.
